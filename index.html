Industries we clean

We clean the places people have to trust

Different premises have different needs. Here is how we work in each.

Offices and professional services

Legal practices, architecture studios, creative agencies, co-working operators and corporate tenancies across Melbourne, and South East Suburbs. Typically two to five services a week, out of hours, with a fixed cleaner so your team sees the same face.

Childcare centres

Childcare is the most scrutinised premises we clean and we build the service around that.

  • WWCC on every cleanerWorking With Children Checks on everyone attending the centre
  • Same cleaner every visitNo rotating strangers through the building
  • Colour coded cleaning suppliesMinimising cross area contamination
  • Photo record every cleanEvidence for your own compliance file
  • LHA licensedSo your engagement of us is answerable
  • Several days a weekAfter hours cleans, photo reports, and frequency based on number of children and staff

Medical and allied health

Consulting rooms, treatment rooms, allied health clinics and studios. Clinical surfaces, contact points and waiting areas are treated to an infection-control routine agreed with the practice, and bathrooms and kitchens are sanitised every visit.

We currently service physio clinics and Pilates studios, where mirrors, equipment and floors have to be spotless every day.

High end retail and showrooms

High end retail shops, display units and showrooms, where the premises are the pitch. Glass, front-of-house and fitting areas cleaned before doors open, so the first thing a customer sees is the product rather than the finish on the floor.

Sporting and community facilities

Sporting clubs, pavilions and community buildings. Change rooms, social rooms, infirmary rooms, kitchens and amenities, cleaned around fixtures and functions rather than office hours.

We work to the club's calendar, turn rooms around after events, and help coordinate and manage the cleaning to facilitate end of season cleans, ensuring local councils sign off at handover to the next season's community teams. We currently clean for community football and cricket clubs in Brighton, Carnegie and McKinnon.

Builders and developers

Final detail cleans that get a completed build ready for handover. We adapt to your build program and send photo reports so you can sign off without attending.

We also send a defect list. If we find something missing or damaged while we clean, a handle off a cupboard, a water tap leaking, a mark on a wall, caulking not finished, we photograph it and send it through, so you can put it right before the client walks in rather than after.
