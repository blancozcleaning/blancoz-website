Industries we clean

We clean the places people have to trust

Different premises have different rules. Here is how we work in each.

Offices and professional services

Legal practices, architecture studios, creative agencies, co-working operators and corporate tenancies across Bayside, South Melbourne, Port Melbourne and Cremorne. Typically two to five services a week, out of hours, with a fixed cleaner so your team sees the same face.

Reference sites include a legal practice we have cleaned for two years and a creative agency we have cleaned for over a year.

Childcare centres

Childcare is the most scrutinised premises we clean and we build the service around that.

  • WWCC on every cleanerWorking With Children Checks on everyone attending the centre
  • Same cleaner every visitNo rotating strangers through the building
  • Photo record every cleanEvidence for your own compliance file
  • LHA licensedSo your engagement of us is answerable
  • Five days a weekSchedules worked around close of care

Medical and allied health

Consulting rooms, treatment rooms and allied health clinics. Clinical surfaces, contact points and waiting areas are treated to an infection-control routine agreed with the practice, and bathrooms and kitchens are sanitised every visit. We currently service a Bayside spine and sports clinic on a fortnightly cycle.

Retail and showrooms

Shopfronts, display units and showrooms, where the premises are the pitch. Glass, front-of-house and fitting areas cleaned before doors open, so the first thing a customer sees is the product rather than the finish on the floor.

Sporting and community facilities

Sporting clubs, pavilions and community buildings. Change rooms, social rooms, kitchens and amenities, cleaned around fixtures and functions rather than office hours.

We work to the club’s calendar, turn rooms around after events, and hold the areas members actually judge you on, the change rooms and the toilets, to a standard they notice. We currently clean for a community football club on a weekly cycle.

Builders and developers

Final detail cleans that get a completed build ready for handover. We adapt to your build program and send photo reports so you can sign off without attending.

We also send a defect list. If we find something missing or damaged while we clean, a handle off a cupboard, a mark on a wall, caulking not finished, we photograph it and send it through, so you can put it right before the client walks in rather than after.
